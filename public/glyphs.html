A duel of drawn spells

Glyphs

Ten seconds a round. Draw your spell in secret; only what it does will be seen. The shapes are not written down.

Give the other caster this code, or send them the link.

Round 1

10

The ink will tell you when something stirs. The cleaner the glyph, the stronger the spell. A glyph inside a circle is a ring to stand behind; the other caster sees the ring, not what it is made of. A strike that gets through wounds; the second wound kills.

The elements

An arrow points at the element it beats. Two arrows that stop each other in the middle quench each other: neither spell lands, and a ring of either stops the other. Two that cross and each reach the other pass through each other: both spells land, and a ring of either gives way.
