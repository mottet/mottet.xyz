The round

    The elements

    An arrow points at the element it beats: the winner's spell goes on, a ring of the winner turns the loser aside, and the winner goes straight through a ring of the loser. Two arrows that stop each other in the middle are a quench: the two spells undo each other and a ring of either turns the other aside. Two arrows that cross and each reach the other element are a pass: both spells go on and each goes straight through a ring of the other. Anything else, and an element against itself, both go on and a ring wears. The matrix below and the ring table follow from this wheel unless a pair is written by hand.

      The spells and their glyphs

        The reaction matrix

        Row: what you cast. Column: what they cast. Each cell says which strikes go on to whom; a strike then meets that caster's ring (see Rings). Rings strike no one and are left out; a fizzle or blank sheet does nothing. A cell in italics is derived from the element wheel; the others are written by hand in the data.
