Self-hosted typefaces for the house pages. Both are published under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 and were downloaded unchanged from Google Fonts (latin subsets):

- Federo (Olexa Volochay, 2011), a revival of Feder Grotesk from 1909, the
  Jugendstil era. Used for titles, door plates, and plan labels.
- Cormorant Garamond (Christian Thalmann), regular and italic. Used for running
  text and captions.
